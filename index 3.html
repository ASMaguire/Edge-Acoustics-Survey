EDGE SERVER ACOUSTICS STUDY

Participant Intake Survey - Retail and Branch Site Workers

Thank you for your interest in this study. Please complete this survey so we can confirm your eligibility and understand your work environment. It takes about 10 minutes.


About the equipment this survey asks about: we are interested in the physical computer servers installed at the location where you work - not laptops, tablets, or cash registers. These are small-to-medium units, roughly the size of a microwave to a small refrigerator, that handle things like transactions, inventory, security cameras, or building systems on site. Throughout this survey we call them "the server equipment."


Your answers are confidential and will be used only to select and group participants for this study. You may skip any question you prefer not to answer.
